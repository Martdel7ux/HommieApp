FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Hommie free to download?
Yes. You can download Hommie and create an account for free.
Where is Hommie available?
We are launching in Cyprus first, with homes across Nicosia, Limassol and Larnaca, and more student cities to follow.
How does student verification work?
Upload a photo of your student ID in the app. Our team reviews it and adds a Verified badge to your profile.
Can I trust the listings?
Landlords create listings in the app and our team reviews them before they go live. If anything looks wrong, you can report it in a tap.
I am a landlord. How do I list my property?
Download the app, sign up as a landlord and follow the five step listing flow. You can also email support@hommieapp.com and we will help you get started.
Which phones does Hommie work on?
Hommie is available for iPhone and Android.
How is my data protected?
We only collect what the app needs to work, and you can request deletion of your account at any time. Read the full Privacy Policy.
